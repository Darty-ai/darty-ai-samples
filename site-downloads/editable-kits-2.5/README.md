# Darty-Ai DIY kits

Download a ZIP, extract it, and open its Excel workbook in Darty-Ai. The design kits
contain **Assets**, **AI Templates** and **Excel Sheets**, plus a short
**START HERE.txt** guide. The utility kits below include export presets and
HTML instructions; Compression works without a spreadsheet. These are ready-to-use Illustrator kits.

Use Adobe Illustrator 2025 or newer with Darty-Ai 2.5. Fonts are listed in the
workbook's **Start here** worksheet; activate them or choose replacements before
exporting. Font files are not distributed with these kits.

## Generate your Illustrator files

1. Extract the complete ZIP to a local folder. Keep its three folders together.
2. In Illustrator, open Darty-Ai and choose **Data**.
3. Load the workbook from **Excel Sheets**. Choose **Darty Designs**, the first sheet.
4. Turn on **Preview** in the traverser table. Select a record or use the
   previous/next controls. The spreadsheet selects the matching template.
5. In Darty-Ai **Export**, select your records, choose Illustrator (`.ai`) and
   an output folder, then export. Save output separately from **AI Templates**.

Edit record values in Excel and reload the saved workbook to make your own
versions. Keep the first four header rows; records start on row five.

The kits work from the extracted folder, including folder names with spaces.
References are relative to the workbook. Sticker illustrations load from Assets into three tagged source placeholders
on the pasteboard, then the table anchors copies into its cells. Templates start with short placeholder text and empty image, table and code
targets. Each layout has at least three spreadsheet variations. Common settings
use row 3 defaults; enter values in records only when they differ. Frame text
replacement uses its implicit mode, so no repeated @ mode is needed.

Food packaging includes two empty box templates and 30 fruit/language records on
one Darty Designs sheet. The 250 × 170 × 85 mm box is the header default for
plums, apricots, nectarines and strawberries; cherry rows override it with the
200 × 136 × 68 mm box. Shared settings also use header defaults, with explicit
row values for exceptions. Fruit artwork loads from Assets when you preview a row. Team apparel
includes two neutral vector layouts with three variations each. The other examples reuse one template
per kit. Packaging is a nominal carton prototype; apparel is placement artwork.
Adapt both to the specifications supplied by your printer or manufacturer.

Brands, people, addresses and credentials are fictional examples. Packaging
nutrition uses USDA FoodData Central values for raw fruit per 100 g, with source
links in the workbook. Tables vary between 9, 10 and 11 rows. The EAN-13 numbers
are checksum-valid demonstration identifiers; assign production identifiers
before commercial use. QR payloads differ by fruit and language.
Photographic mockups illustrate the designs; the native Illustrator files are the
editable artwork. Original source files are retained by the maintainers.

## Review PDFs and mockups

Each PDF opens with a mockup page, followed by the current Illustrator artwork
variations. Apparel shows both layouts together. Packaging shows all five fruit
mockups, then plums in English, German and French and the other four fruits in
English. Its Excel workbook retains all 30 fruit/language combinations.

Packaging mockups use Darty-Ai Box Maker with registered artwork exported from
Illustrator. The box proportions and print placement come from the document.
Separate image-tool realism experiments are excluded from these PDFs.
The contact sheets are high-resolution PNG grids, compressed with Darty-Ai
optimizeBitmap. The contact grids are included as separate downloads.

## Table workflow update — October 25, 2026

Packaging, Product catalogues, Menus, Property and Stickers now use readable
`column[A]` data and indexed range rules. Table dimensions come from the content;
shared text, padding and shrink-to-frame settings use header defaults. Header,
column and cell rules retain the individual designs. The E-bike price row uses
the footer rule, and sticker illustrations remain attached to their cells.

Packaging's table, barcode and QR inherit the existing 180-degree template
placeholders. The workbook no longer overrides the table's rotation. Artwork
settings across all kits use at most one decimal place, with whole or half units
where practical. Prices, nutrition, barcode/QR payloads and other content retain
their original values.

Five ZIPs and their matching review PDFs/contact grids are refreshed. The other
seven kits needed no content changes. Source templates, assets and utility
presets remain unchanged. Validation uses Illustrator 2026 on macOS and Darty-Ai
**2.5.0.6429** and the October 25 CEP spreadsheet-range fix. Use a Darty-Ai
installation containing those fitting/range fixes for the refreshed table kits.
All 66 workbook records passed Preview, and all ten design kits exported AI
files. See the [validation record](validation-20261025.json) and
[verified downloads](download-verification-20261025.json). Windows runtime
is not qualified by these checks.

## Historical compatibility checkpoint — October 14, 2026

That checkpoint used Darty-Ai native **2.5.0.6406** in Illustrator 2026 on macOS.
The 63 workbook records used 12 minimal templates. Native Preview checks covered
all 33 non-packaging records and the seven packaging editions shown in the PDF,

## Compression and XMP utility kits

- [Compression DIY Kit](https://github.com/Darty-ai/darty-ai-samples/releases/download/editable-kits-2.5/darty-ai-compression.zip): three embedded original images, on-artboard instructions and a two-job preset producing PNG/JPEG pairs with compression off and on. [View the measured comparison PDF](https://github.com/Darty-ai/darty-ai-samples/releases/download/editable-kits-2.5/darty-ai-compression.pdf).
- [XMP metadata DIY Kit](https://github.com/Darty-ai/darty-ai-samples/releases/download/editable-kits-2.5/darty-ai-xmp.zip): a three-record workbook using named variables from the fourth header row, images, matching five- and four-star templates, and a preset exporting PNG, JPEG and PDF. The examples include creators, locations, ratings 5/5/4 and fictional studio contact details. Open exported files in Adobe Bridge to inspect their XMP.

For these kits, extract the ZIP and open **START HERE.html** before importing
the supplied export preset. Compression's source images are deliberately
embedded at their original resolution. XMP uses Merge mode to inherit each
template's rating while setting text fields from the spreadsheet. The blue
instruction panel stays outside the exported artboard.
